Our Mission Statement

We are Team 11, a group of UNC Charlotte students who bring together skills in areas like backend development, frontend development, data science, systems & network engineering, and cybersecurity to build reliable software. We work collaboratively, review each other's code, and share what we learn so that every member grows with each project. Our mission is to turn what we learn in the classroom into work we're proud of, while building the habits of a strong professional team.
